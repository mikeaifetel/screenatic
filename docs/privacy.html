Privacy policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Screenatic is a screen-mirroring receiver app for TVs. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with data.

What we don't collect

  • No account, sign-in, name, email or phone number.
  • No analytics or tracking SDKs, and no advertising identifiers.
  • What you cast (your screen, videos, music, photos) is played on your TV and is never uploaded to us or anyone else.

What stays on your TV

  • A random device identifier created on first launch, used to identify the TV to your phone on your local network and to keep feature settings consistent. It is not linked to you and is not sent to us.
  • App settings such as volume and your remaining cast time.

Network connections

  • Local network: the app announces itself on your Wi-Fi (Bonjour/mDNS and SSDP) so your devices can find it, and receives the content they send. This traffic stays inside your network.
  • Media links: when an app casts a link (for example a video URL), the TV downloads it directly from that app's server, as any media player would.
  • App configuration: the app periodically downloads a small settings file from screenatic.app. Like any web request, this reveals your IP address to our hosting provider (Cloudflare), which may keep standard server logs. We do not use this to identify you.

Advertising

The current version shows no ads. If a future version adds ads, this policy will be updated before that version is released, describing the ad provider and the data it uses.

Children

Screenatic is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/mikeaifetel/screenatic/issues.
